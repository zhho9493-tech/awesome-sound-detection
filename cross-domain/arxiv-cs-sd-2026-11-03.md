### 1. Wave Physics as Representation: Tensor-Structured Neural Reconstruction for Ocean Acoustic Fields

- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01680
- **PDF**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.01680
- **作者**: Yifan Sun; Panqi Chen; Weifeng Shi; Xiao Fu; Lei Cheng
- **技术类别**: physics-structured representation
- **论文摘要层面的贡献**: TIDAL derives a structured spatial-angular tensor representation from wave physics; learns under uncertain environment parameters instead of requiring a fully specified wave equation.
- **潜在迁移（未经验证）**: 可探索传播物理诱导的低秩空间表示，辅助DAS多探测器跨项目建模；海洋声场结论不能直接视作光纤验证。
- **代码/复现状态**: 未核验官方代码；尚未复现。
